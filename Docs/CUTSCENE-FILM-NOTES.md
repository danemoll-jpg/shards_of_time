# Cutscenes — what Dan's film actually shows

Dan's clips (2026-11-11) are in `SourceArt/Reference/Clips/` (720p MP4 copies of his originals). **The clip is canon
for its scene** (`Docs/Design/design-decisions.md`). Notes by the planning chat from frames of each clip; times are
seconds into the clip. Pull your own frames (`ffmpeg -i clip.mp4 -vf fps=2 …`) and match the staging: shot order,
camera angles, framing, who does what, timing. Game style and game models (Dan's concept sheets), not the film's
look. Outfits follow the game's concept sheets, not the clips.

## 00-Home_ActOne.mp4 (64 s; the game's `ActOne` is 19 s now)
- 0–13: living room, purple walls, red curtains, lit tree. Dan and Abi in Christmas pyjamas **decorating the tree**
  together; Kira plays under it. Dan holds the dark bauble; Kira swats at it; Dan fumbles it.
- 13–19: the ornament drops; Dan lunges for it, misses; Abi gasps.
- 21–30: close on the floor: the ornament **glows gold and cracks**, light beams shoot out of the cracks; Kira sniffs
  it; Dan and Abi kneel, Abi scoops Kira up.
- 30–40: blue crystal shards burst out and fly past; a **rainbow spiral vortex** opens on the floor and grows.
- 40–48: Dan, Abi (holding Kira) are sucked in **holding hands**, tumbling into the spiral; full-screen rainbow swirl.
- 49–64: **into the Ice Age:** a mammoth in the sky shot, a blue vortex spinning among clouds, the three fall out of it
  over snowy mountains and erupting volcanoes, and land in the snow among mammoths near a firelit cave.
- So Act I runs straight into the Ice Age arrival; the game splits it at the landing (`ActOne` ends with the fall,
  `IceAgeArrival` starts on landing).

## 06-Steampunk_Arrival.mp4 (25 s)
- 0–14 s: night exterior of a steampunk industrial city (domes, chimneys, pipes, lit windows), a giant brass gear
  wheel in the foreground puffing steam. A teal ribbon streaks across the sky and winds into a big flat teal spiral
  vortex above the factory.
- 14–18 s: pushes in over the domes toward the factory.
- 18–25 s: inside, a gear wall with a chain conveyor and steam vents above a copper bucket full of bolts. The four
  (Abi, Dan, Muscle Man, mechanical Kira) drop out of the vortex onto the chain line with arms up, then land in the
  bolt bucket, which a press arm lifts and carries along the chain. (Stills: `Worlds/06-Steampunk/bucket-arrival.png`,
  `bolt-bucket.png`.)

## 06-Steampunk_MuscleManHero.mp4 (12 s)
- 0–7 s: the bucket has tipped them onto a blue-roller conveyor. Abi, Dan and Kira tumble along it, flat on their
  bellies; Dan and Abi cover their heads; Muscle Man falls in behind.
- 7–10 s: the big brass dome press comes down over the belt. Muscle Man stands under it and holds it up with both arms;
  alarms flash red, cables snap, sparks.
- 10–12 s: he heaves it off: the press dome flies through the factory wall and window, glass bursting, the hall full
  of dust and smoke (`factory-broken-wall.png`). This is his real hero moment (he finally saves Dan, not the reverse).

Note: the film's city exterior is night; the street stills are daytime. The game can do the factory at night and the
streets at dawn/day after the escape.

## What the game should change (for the cutscene redo round)
1. `ActOne`: follow the clip: decorating together, Dan fumbles the bauble, the glow / crack / shard burst, the
   rainbow spiral, holding hands, then the fall from the blue vortex over the Ice Age.
2. `IceAgeArrival`: the on-screen transformation by the campfire; the shot pattern.
3. `EgyptArrival`: the establishing shot, the red vortex, the sand plume and pause, digging for Kira, mummy Kira pops up.
4. `SphinxGag`: push-in, held close-ups, mummy Kira's innocent look.
5. `BethlehemArrival`: the Wise Men see the "star" turn into the ring; the crash; Abi on the hay, Dan on the floor.
6. `WiseMenArrive`: the doors, the manger, disappointment, the tossed present, the hay tornado.
7. `MedievalArrival`: the purple vortex over the castle, top-down drop into the wagon, Kira flying after.
8. `TheStone`: the challenge, the effortless pull, "My hero!" (not "Master!"), **the cobblestone whirlpool** (not a
   plain purple vortex) taking everyone including Muscle Man.
9. Pirate Seas (Round 10): `PirateArrival` at night with lanterns, a green vortex round the ship, barrels, Muscle Man
   through the sails.
